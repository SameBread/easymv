A/V sync test kit (§8.3)

Generates a 10 s master that is a 1 Hz click track, a white image, and a project that flashes the image with each click. Load all three into EasyMV, export, then check that each flash frame lines up with its click (required: ≤ 1 frame). A positive/negative measured offset can be fed back with localStorage.setItem('easymv.audioOffsetMs', '<ms>') (positive delays audio).
